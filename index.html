Frequently Asked Questions

Questions and Answers

Are the terms of the Lease Negotiable?

Yes, many of the terms would be negotiable, like the rent and the lease term.

What is the zoning, and what types of businesses are allowed?

According to Town of Marlborough Zoning Code § 155-12 (Article IV), the HD district outlines specific rules for commercial and light industrial operations: Light Industrial & Manufacturing: Activities focused on the manufacture, assemblage, treatment, or packaging of products are permitted, provided they operate completely without public hazard or nuisances like excessive noise, smoke, dust, or odors.Warehousing & Storage: Wholesale and Storage establishments are allowed, but they must be conducted inside completely enclosed buildings (open storage is allowed only if fully screened by an opaque fence or wall at least 8 feet high. Agricultural Processing (Special Use): Light industrial businesses focused specifically on manufacturing, processing, packaging, or warehousing agricultural Products can be permitted via a special use process. Commercial and Retail: Retail stores, shopping centers, commercial groups, and Business/professional offices are widely permitted to support the highway corridor.

What is the monthly lease rate?

$35,833.33 plus CAM and utilities.

What is the minimum lease term length?

5 Years.

Are there loading docks and high-clearance doors?

Yes, the facility includes loading docks and high-clearance doors, featuring 4 assigned docks and a bay space 4 feet away.

How is the location for commuter and freight access?

The building's location offers strong regional connectivity via US-9W and nearby major river crossings, making it well-suited for light manufacturing and warehousing.
